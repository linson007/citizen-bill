MattamUndo01

The gap

Citizens have ideas. The system needs usable proposals.

In Kerala, private member bills exist as a path for legislative ideas, but public participation around drafting is still hard to organize.

1

Ideas stay informal

Public concerns often remain as posts, petitions, or conversations.

2

Drafting is inaccessible

Most people do not know how to convert a problem into bill language.

3

Support is scattered

Votes, comments, evidence, and revisions rarely live in one place.

4

Good proposals are invisible

Representatives and civic groups need clearer public-backed signals.

Problem02

The platform

A public participation layer for legislative drafting.

MattamUndo combines structured creation, AI drafting support, community feedback, and public discovery into one open platform.

Sign in

Google login gives every proposal a real author and contribution history.

Create

Structured fields guide users from problem to solution to public impact.

Improve

AI helps draft, simplify, summarize, translate, and stress-test ideas.

Discuss

People vote, comment, suggest amendments, and share proposals publicly.

Track

Dashboards surface trending, supported, recent, and Kerala-wide public bills.

Progress04

Why AI belongs here

AI reduces the drafting barrier, but never replaces review.

The assistant is intentionally scoped to civic and public bill work. It helps users structure ideas while refusing unrelated or unsafe requests.

Guardrails

Prompts are checked for civic relevance and unsafe content before AI work.

Consent

AI conversation history is saved only when the user chooses to keep it.

Limits

Daily per-user limits protect API costs and reduce platform abuse.

Disclaimer

Every AI output is treated as a draft for public discussion, not legal advice.
