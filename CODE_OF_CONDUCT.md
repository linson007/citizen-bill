# Code of Conduct

Citizen Bill is a civic technology project. Contributors and users are expected to participate respectfully and constructively.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of different political, legal, social, and regional views.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep discussions relevant and evidence-oriented.
- Avoid harassment, threats, hate speech, and personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not expose personal data without consent.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or intimidation.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information.
- Spam or bad-faith disruption.
- Impersonation.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community.
